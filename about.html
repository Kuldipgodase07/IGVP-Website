ABOUT IGVP

Building Intelligence, Trust and Technology for a Connected World.

IGVP — Indigenous Global Venture Partners Inc. — brings together artificial intelligence, data, digital trust, finance, healthcare and technology consulting to build intelligent solutions for organizations navigating a rapidly changing digital world.

Discover Our Story →

Technology should help organizations move forward, not make them more complex. Our founders and core team have spent years working across technology, data, analytics, finance, artificial intelligence, and enterprise transformation. Across industries and geographies, we kept seeing the same challenge — organizations had technology, data, and tools, but often struggled to connect them into practical solutions that create real value.

That gap is why IGVP exists. We came together to build a technology and venture organization that doesn’t simply deliver projects, but creates intelligent solutions, scalable platforms, and innovative products that solve real-world problems. We bring together AI, data engineering, healthcare, finance, Edge PKI, digital trust, and technology consulting to help organizations build for what comes next.

We focus on innovation, intelligence, trust, and measurable impact — because technology matters most when it creates meaningful outcomes and continues to deliver value in the real world.

Dr. Shailesh Chavan Founder & Managing Director, IGVP

Story Behind
the Name – IGVP

Data is rarely the problem. Lack of structure is. Most organizations have plenty of signals, but they’re scattered, inconsistent, and hard to trust. That’s when decisions slow down.

IGVP (Indigenous Global Venture Partners) stands for balance, proportion, and disciplined architecture – the principle that strong systems don’t happen by accident. They follow a structure that holds steady even when complexity grows.

That’s what IGVP represents: a disciplined way to bring order to messy data, turn it into dependable intelligence, and help teams make decisions with confidence. In short: less noise, more clarity, so outcomes aren’t luck, they’re repeatable.

Our Purpose

It is simple: To turn enterprise data into adopted decisions, safely, clearly, and without lock-in. We don’t measure success by dashboards delivered or models built. We measure success by real usage, real decisions, and real business impact. We exist to make data and AI practical, usable, and trustworthy, so leaders can decide with confidence.

Our Values

Our values guide how we work, especially when things get complex. These values aren’t slogans. They show up in how we scope work, run sprints, review outcomes, and stay involved long after go-live.

Clarity over jargon

We say things simply and focus on what matters.

Pragmatism over theory

We build the smallest thing that works and build from there.

Accountability over promises

Clear plans, visible progress, and shared responsibility.

Ethics over shortcuts

No gimmicks. No hidden risks. No forced lock-in.

Partnership over transactions

We work alongside our clients, not above them.

Pride in adoption

If people don’t use what we build, we haven’t done our job.
